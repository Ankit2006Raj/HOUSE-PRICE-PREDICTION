About Smart Property

Revolutionizing real estate valuation with artificial intelligence and green standards

Our Mission

To democratize access to accurate real estate valuations by leveraging cutting-edge machine learning technology. We believe everyone should have access to transparent, data-driven pricing models when making decisions on eco-friendly and energy-efficient homes.

Our Vision

To become the world's most trusted AI platform for eco-friendly real estate valuation, empowering green builders and homeowners to realize the true premium of sustainable design and material choices.

Machine Learning Models

  • Linear Regression: Baseline pricing model based on basic square footage.
  • Ridge & Lasso Regression: Regularized regressions to penalize noisy variables.
  • Random Forest Ensemble: Non-linear model grouping multi-level decisions for location factors.
  • Gradient Boosting: Advanced boosting algorithm providing high-precision fitting for final predictions.
